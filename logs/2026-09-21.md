## Checkpoint: Root Cause Analysis & Proposed Fix
- **timestamp:** 2026-09-21T10:22:00+03:00
- **what was done:** Performed end-to-end audit of `chrome-extension/technoreboot-avito/content.js` gallery extraction pipeline. Identified four compounding flaws causing multi-photo listings to collapse to 1 photo:
  1. `findGalleryRootElement()` returned `image-frame` when outer container markers differed, isolating the query scope and preventing discovery of sibling thumbnails.
  2. `thumbSelectors` were overly restricted and missed modern Avito layout variants (`[class*="gallery-list"]`, `div > *`, etc.).
  3. `walkAndCollectAllGalleryPhotos` next-button traversal was blocked behind `targetN > 0`, but desktop Avito lacks counter text by default (`targetN === 0`), skipping traversal completely when thumbnails were unmounted.
  4. Truncation in `extractAllPhotos` clamped traversed photos to `expectedPhotoCount === 1`.
